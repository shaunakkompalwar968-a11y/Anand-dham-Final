About Our Sanctuary

A 31-Acre Natural Haven for Families, Schools & Corporates

Recognized by the Government and founded by Dr. Kompalwar, Anand Dham Agrotourism was born from a passion to reconnect families and children with the roots of Indian village culture, pure natural air, and organic wellness.

Located on the tranquil banks of Sitakhandi Lake near Bhokar, our retreat offers the perfect antidote to urban stress — with zero pollution, fresh farm breezes, and the warm hospitality of rural Maharashtra.
